=== 4.1.1
The formula: $\exists x\forall y[\neg\exists z(p(y,z)\wedge p(z,y)) \equiv p(x,y)]$.

1. **Eliminate ≡:** $(A\to B)\wedge(B\to A)$, where $A = \neg\exists z(p(y,z)\wedge p(z,y))$ and $B = p(x,y)$.
2. **Skolemise $\exists x$** with a constant $a$. Push the negation inward: $A = \forall z\,\neg(p(y,z)\wedge p(z,y))$.
   - $A\to B$: $\neg A\vee B = \exists z(p(y,z)\wedge p(z,y))\vee p(a,y)$. Skolemise this $z$ as $f(y)$.
   - $B\to A$: $\neg p(a,y)\vee\forall z(\neg p(y,z)\vee\neg p(z,y))$.

**Clausal form** (all variables universally quantified):

1. $p(y, f(y))\vee p(a,y)$
2. $p(f(y), y)\vee p(a,y)$
3. $\neg p(a,y)\vee\neg p(y,z)\vee\neg p(z,y)$
=== 4.1.2
Use the clauses from (i).

1. **Set $y = a$ in clause 3:** $\neg p(a,a)\vee\neg p(a,z)\vee\neg p(z,a)$. With $z = a$, this becomes **$\neg p(a,a)$** (C4).
2. **Clause 1 with $y = a$:** $p(a,f(a))\vee p(a,a)$. Resolve with C4: **$p(a,f(a))$** (C5).
3. **Clause 2 with $y = a$:** $p(f(a),a)\vee p(a,a)$. Resolve with C4: **$p(f(a),a)$** (C6).
4. **Clause 3 with $y = a$, $z = f(a)$:** $\neg p(a,a)\vee\neg p(a,f(a))\vee\neg p(f(a),a)$. Resolve with C5 and C6: $\neg p(a,a)$. That's consistent with C4 so far, so take a different instance.
5. **Clause 3 with $y = f(a)$:** $\neg p(a,f(a))\vee\neg p(f(a),z)\vee\neg p(z,f(a))$. With $z = a$: $\neg p(a,f(a))\vee\neg p(f(a),a)\vee\neg p(a,f(a))$. Resolve with C5 and C6: **□ (the empty clause)**.

The empty clause is derived, so the formula is **unsatisfiable**.
