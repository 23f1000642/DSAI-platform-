=== 4.4.36
Over the integers:

- **(A)** $\forall n\,\exists m\,(n^2 < m)$: take $m = n^2 + 1$. **True.**
- **(B)** $\exists n\,\forall m\,(n < m^2)$: $m = 0$ needs $n < 0$, so take $n = -1$. Then every $m^2 \ge 0 > -1$. **True.**
- **(C)** $n = 1$ gives $1\cdot m = m$. **True.**
- **(D)** $n^2 + m^2 = 6$ has no integer solutions. **False.**
- **(E)** $n + m = 4$ and $n - m = 1$ give $n = 2.5$, which isn't an integer. **False.**

So **(A), (B) and (C)** are true. That's choice **(1)**, **option A**.
