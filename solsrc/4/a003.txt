=== 4.4.23
$\varphi$ says: for every $x$ whose only divisors are $x$ and 1 (a prime), there's a larger such $w$. In other words, every prime has a larger prime.

- **$S_1$ (1–100) fails.** 97 is prime, but no larger prime exists in $S_1$.
- **$S_2$ (positive integers) satisfies it:** there are infinitely many primes.
- **$S_3$ (all integers) satisfies it vacuously.** Every $x$ is divisible by −1, so the condition "the only divisors are $x$ and 1" is never true. The implication holds for every $x$.

**$S_2$ and $S_3$**, **option C**.
