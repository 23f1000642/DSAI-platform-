=== 1.65.34
$\lg^* n$ is the iterated logarithm, and it grows extremely slowly. Compare:

- $\lg^*(\lg n) = \lg^* n - 1$
- $\lg^*(n!) \approx \lg^*(n\lg n) \approx \lg^* n$ (+1 at most)
- $\lg(n!) = \Theta(n\lg n)$, which is huge
- $\lg(\lg^* n)$ is the logarithm of an already tiny value, so it's the smallest

**Option A**.
