=== 1.42.4
**(a) Weight $|u-v|$.** Every edge weighs at least 1, and a spanning tree needs $n-1$ edges. The **path** $0-1-2-\dots-(n-1)$ uses only weight-1 edges, so it's an MST, with weight $n-1$.

**(b) Weight $u+v$.** Connect every vertex to 0: the **star** with edges $(0,v)$. Vertex $v$ must be joined by some edge, and the cheapest edge touching $v$ is $(0,v)$ with weight $v$. Taking that for every $v$ gives a spanning tree. Its weight is
$$1 + 2 + \dots + (n-1) = \frac{n(n-1)}{2}$$
and it's minimal.
