=== 11.6.4
Degeneracy arises when there are **fewer** than $m + n - 1$ allocations (ii), or when several cells empty at once during improvement (iii). It doesn't occur when there are exactly $m + n - 1$ allocations (iv).

The official key is **C, (i) and (iv)**.
