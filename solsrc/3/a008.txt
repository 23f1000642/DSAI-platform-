=== 3.20.2
**Armstrong's axioms:**

1. **Reflexivity:** if $Y\subseteq X$, then $X\to Y$.
2. **Augmentation:** if $X\to Y$, then $XZ\to YZ$ for any $Z$.
3. **Transitivity:** if $X\to Y$ and $Y\to Z$, then $X\to Z$.

They are sound (they only derive valid FDs) and complete (they derive every FD that's implied).
=== 3.20.3
Derive $x\to yz$ (the union rule):

1. $x\to y$ (given). Augment with $x$: $x\to xy$.
2. $x\to z$ (given). Augment with $y$: $xy\to yz$.
3. Transitivity on steps 1 and 2: $x\to xy\to yz$, so **$x\to yz$**.
=== 3.20.4
Derive $xw\to z$ (pseudo-transitivity):

1. $x\to y$ (given). Augment with $w$: $xw\to yw$.
2. $wy\to z$ (given).
3. Transitivity: $xw\to wy\to z$, so **$xw\to z$**.
=== 3.20.5
Derive $x\to z$ (the decomposition rule):

1. $z\subseteq y$, so by reflexivity $y\to z$.
2. $x\to y$ (given).
3. Transitivity: **$x\to z$**.
