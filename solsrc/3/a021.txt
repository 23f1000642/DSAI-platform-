=== 3.50.7
Deleting employee 20 cascades to everyone whose manager_id is 20, and then recursively:

1. Manager 20 leads to **(35, 20)** being deleted.
2. Manager 35 leads to **(30, 35)** being deleted.
3. Manager 30: nobody reports to 30.

So (30, 35) and (35, 20) only, **option B**.

=== 3.51.8
For each course, the student must have completed every one of its prerequisites. Use **division**:
$$\Pi_{Cno,\,Pre\_Cno}(PRE\_REQ)\ \div\ \rho_{Pre\_Cno}\Big(\Pi_{Cno}\big(\sigma_{Student\_no=2310}(COMPLETED)\big)\Big)$$
The division returns the courses whose prerequisite set is contained in the courses 2310 has completed.

(It returns only courses that have at least one prerequisite. To include courses with none, union with $\Pi_{Cno}(COURSES) - \Pi_{Cno}(PRE\_REQ)$.)
