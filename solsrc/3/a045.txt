=== 3.57.43
Precedence graphs:

- **S1:** r1 before w2 gives T1→T2. r2 before w1 gives T2→T1. Cycle. ✘
- **S2:** r1 before w2 gives T1→T2. r2 before w1 gives T2→T1. Cycle. ✘
- **S3:** r1 before w2 gives T1→T2. r2 before w1 gives T2→T1. Cycle. ✘
- **S4:** r2 and w2 come before r1, r3 and w1. Edges T2→T3, T2→T1 and T3→T1. Acyclic ✔ (order T2, T3, T1).

**Option D**.
=== 3.57.44

=== 3.58.2
Two operations **conflict** when all three of these hold:

- (a) at least one of them is a **write**
- (b) they act on the **same** data item
- (c) they belong to **different** transactions
