=== 3.51.34
a: 16
A cartesian product pairs every tuple of X with every tuple of Y: $4\times4 = \mathbf{16}$ tuples. That's choice **(1)**.
=== 3.51.35
a: 3
An equi-join on X.S = Y.S keeps the pairs whose S values match. X has S values J, B, R, T, and Y has J, B, R, A. The common values are **J, B, R**, so there are **3** tuples. That's choice **(2)**.
=== 3.51.36
A **full outer join** keeps every tuple from **both** X and Y. Unmatched tuples get NULLs for the other side's attributes:

- T from X gets nulls for P and D
- A from Y gets nulls for Si and C

That's choice **(4)**.
