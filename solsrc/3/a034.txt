The attributes are independent, so:
$$P(A>10 \vee B=18) = \frac23 + \frac1{20} - \frac23\cdot\frac1{20} = \frac{40+3-2}{60} = \frac{41}{60}$$
The estimate is $1200\times\frac{41}{60} = \mathbf{820}$.
