=== 3.51.38
Each of these returns the R tuples whose B value appears in S:

- **I:** $\pi_{A,B}(R\bowtie S)$
- **II:** $R\bowtie\pi_B(S)$
- **III:** $R\cap(\pi_A(R)\times\pi_B(S))$. An R tuple $(a,b)$ is in the product exactly when $b\in\pi_B(S)$.

**IV**, $\pi_{A,R.B}(R\times S)$, returns **all** of R whenever S is non-empty, so it's different.

**I, II and III**, **option D**.
