3NF (in its classic form) means no transitive dependency: **no non-prime attribute depends on other non-prime attributes**. 2NF already rules out dependence on part of a key. **Option A**.
=== 3.41.2
a: D
- **$R_1(A,B,C)$**: the key is A, and every FD has A on the left. So it's in BCNF, and therefore in **3NF**.
- **$R_2(A,D,E)$**: the key is A. D → E is transitive, so it's **2NF but not 3NF**.
