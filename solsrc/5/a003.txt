=== 5.9.1
At the jump $x = \pi$, the Fourier series converges to the average of the left and right limits: $\frac{\pi + 0}{2} = \frac\pi2$.

There, $\cos n\pi = (-1)^n$, so $(\cos n\pi - 1)\cos n\pi = 1 - (-1)^n$. That's 2 for odd n and 0 for even n. The sine terms vanish. So
$$\frac\pi2 = \frac\pi4 + \frac2\pi\sum_{n\text{ odd}}\frac{1}{n^2} \Rightarrow \sum_{n\text{ odd}}\frac1{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{8}$$
**Option C**.
=== 5.9.2
