=== 7.9.3
A is upper triangular with eigenvalues $1, -1, i, -i$. Its characteristic polynomial is
$$(\lambda - 1)(\lambda + 1)(\lambda - i)(\lambda + i) = (\lambda^2 - 1)(\lambda^2 + 1) = \lambda^4 - 1$$

=== 7.9.5
Let $M = \begin{pmatrix}1&0&1\\-1&1&1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix}$. Then $\det M = 1(0 - 1) - 0 + 1(-1 - 0) = -2$.

The cofactors give the adjugate:
$$\operatorname{adj}M = \begin{pmatrix}-1&1&-1\\0&0&-2\\-1&-1&1\end{pmatrix}$$
So
$$M^{-1} = \frac{\operatorname{adj}M}{\det M} = \begin{pmatrix}\tfrac12&-\tfrac12&\tfrac12\\0&0&1\\\tfrac12&\tfrac12&-\tfrac12\end{pmatrix}$$
Check: $M M^{-1} = I$ ✔.
