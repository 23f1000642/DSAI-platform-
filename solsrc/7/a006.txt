=== 7.17.3
**Forward elimination.** At step $k$ ($k = 1..n-1$), each of the $n-k$ rows below the pivot needs one multiplier, plus updates to $n-k$ coefficients and the right-hand side. That's $(n-k)(n-k+1)$ multiply-add operations:
$$\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}(n-k)(n-k+1) = \sum_{j=1}^{n-1}j(j+1) = \frac{(n-1)n(n+1)}{3} = \frac{n^3 - n}{3}$$

**Back substitution.** Solving for $x_i$ takes $n-i$ multiply-adds (plus one division):
$$\sum_{i=1}^{n}(n-i) = \frac{n(n-1)}{2}$$

**Total:** about $\frac{n^3}{3} + \frac{n^2}{2} - \frac{5n}{6}$, which is $\approx\frac{n^3}{3}$, i.e. $O(n^3)$. (Counting the $n$ divisions as well adds $n$.)
