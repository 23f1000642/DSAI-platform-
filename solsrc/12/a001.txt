=== 12.3.1
a: A

=== 12.8.1
- **A is true.** A language has an LR(0) grammar if and only if it's accepted by a DPDA and has the prefix property.
- **B is false.** A single-tape TM simulates n moves of a multitape TM in $O(n^2)$, not $n^3$.
- **C is false.** A PDA with one stack is weaker than a TM. It takes **two** stacks to match a TM.
- **D is true.** $a^nb^nc^n$ is context-sensitive but not context-free.

The true statements are **A and D**. No option lists exactly that, so this question has no official key.
