=== 13.28.2
**B: 3, 4, 5, 2, 1.**

1. Push 1, 2, 3, then pop 3.
2. Push 4, pop 4.
3. Push 5, pop 5.
4. Pop 2, then pop 1.

✔

The others fail:

- **A:** after 3, 4, 5, the top of the stack is 2, so 1 can't come out next.
- **C:** 1, 5 is fine, but then the top is 4, so 2 can't come out.
- **D:** after 5, 4, 3, the top is 2, so 1 can't come out.

**Option B**.
=== 13.28.3
$A + \big((B * (C+D)) / F\big) + (D * E)$.

The postfix form is **A B C D + \* F / + D E \* +**.

Option B, ABCD+\*F/DE\*++, is the right-grouped version, $A + (\dots/F + D*E)$. Addition is associative, so it gives the same value, and it's the only valid choice. **Option B**.
=== 13.28.4
**(a)** Keep the queue's **rear at the top** of the stack.

- **ENQUEUE(x)** = PUSH(x), which is 1 operation.
- **DEQUEUE** = REVERSE, POP, REVERSE, which is 3 operations. The front comes to the top, gets popped, and the order is restored.

**(b)** Evaluate 5 2 \* 3 4 + 5 2 \* \* +:

- (i) After `5 2 * 3 4 +`, the stack is **[10, 7]**.
- (ii) After `… 5 2`, the stack is **[10, 7, 5, 2]**.
- (iii) Then \* gives [10, 7, 10], \* gives [10, 70], and + gives **[80]**. The final value is 80.
=== 13.28.5
Element $m$ is pushed $m$-th and popped $(n-m+1)$-th among the pops. Between the end of Push($m$) and the start of its pop, there are:

- $n - m$ later pushes
- $n - m$ earlier pops

So there are $2(n-m)$ operations, each taking $X$, plus $2(n-m)+1$ gaps of $Y$:
$$\text{life}(m) = 2(n-m)(X+Y) + Y$$
Averaging over $m = 1..n$, the mean of $(n-m)$ is $\frac{n-1}{2}$:
$$(n-1)(X+Y) + Y = n(X+Y) - X$$
**Option C**.
