=== 13.31.1
In-order is left, node, right:

- Left subtree of 15: 4's subtree gives 2 4 3, then 13, then 7's subtree gives 7 6 (6 is 7's right child).
- Then 15.
- Right subtree: 17, 20, then 18's subtree gives 18 18 (the lower 18 is the left child of the upper 18).

Result: **2 4 3 13 7 6 15 17 20 18 18**, **option D**.
