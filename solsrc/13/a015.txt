=== 13.17.19
The $i$-th insertion may scan $i$ nodes to find its place:
$$\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \Theta(n^2)$$
**Option C**.
=== 13.17.20
Reverse the list in place with three pointers (prev, curr, next): one pass, $O(1)$ space. Every node has to be visited, so **$\Theta(n)$** is optimal. **Option A**.
=== 13.17.21
- **SLLdel** has to find the predecessor by walking from the head: $O(n)$.
- **DLLdel** gets the predecessor from the prev pointer: $O(1)$.

**Option D**.

=== 13.17.23
Copy the next node's data into $x$ and delete the next node instead. That's $O(1)$.

But if $x$ is the **last** node, there's no next node to copy from. Then you must find the predecessor, which takes $O(n)$. The worst case is **$O(n)$**, **option A**.
