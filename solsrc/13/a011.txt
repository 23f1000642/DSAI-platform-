=== 13.13.3
**(a)** Each insertion misses bucket 1 with probability $\frac{n-1}{n}$, independently:
$$\left(\frac{n-1}{n}\right)^K$$

**(b)** No collision means all $K$ keys land in distinct buckets:
$$\frac{n(n-1)(n-2)\cdots(n-K+1)}{n^K}$$

**(c)** The first $K-1$ keys are in distinct buckets, and the $K$-th hits one of those $K-1$ occupied buckets:
$$\frac{n(n-1)\cdots(n-K+2)}{n^{K-1}}\cdot\frac{K-1}{n}$$
