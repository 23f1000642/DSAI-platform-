=== 13.13.8
Work out which keys must come before which:

- **42, 23, 34** sit in their home slots, so each just needs its slot to be free when it arrives.
- **46** sits in its home slot 6. It must arrive before 52 and 33 could probe past slot 5 into 6.
- **52** (home 2, placed at 5) needs slots 2, 3 and 4 filled first, so 42, 23 and 34 come before it.
- **33** (home 3, placed at 7) needs slots 3 to 6 filled first, so 23, 34, 52 and 46 come before it.

So 33 is last. 52 comes after {42, 23, 34}, which can be in any order (3! = 6). 46 can go anywhere before 33, giving 5 positions in a sequence of 5.

That's $6\times5 = \mathbf{30}$ sequences, **option C**.
