=== 9.17.12
A sum of 22 with four dice means a shortfall of $24 - 22 = 2$ below the maximum (all sixes). Spread the shortfall of 2 over 4 dice:

- one die short by 2: $\{4, 6, 6, 6\}$, in 4 arrangements
- two dice short by 1: $\{5, 5, 6, 6\}$, in $\binom42 = 6$ arrangements

$X = 4 + 6 = \mathbf{10}$.

=== 9.17.17
A trial is a tie with probability $\tfrac16$. Winning on the third trial means two ties, then no tie:
$$\left(\tfrac16\right)^2\cdot\tfrac56 = \frac{5}{216}\approx\mathbf{0.023}$$
=== 9.17.18
a: A
This is Pólya's urn. By symmetry, the probability of drawing red is the same on every trial: $\frac{r}{r+b}$.

Check for trial 2: $\frac{r}{r+b}\cdot\frac{r+1}{r+b+1} + \frac{b}{r+b}\cdot\frac{r}{r+b+1} = \frac{r(r+b+1)}{(r+b)(r+b+1)} = \frac{r}{r+b}$ ✔.

**Option A**.
